Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

Overview

Local Like a Local is a privacy-first application. We do not collect, transmit, or store your personal data on any server. This policy explains exactly what happens with your data.

Data We Store

All data is stored locally in your browser's localStorage and IndexedDB. This includes:

  • Favorites: Venues you've saved (stored as a JSON array in localStorage)
  • Preferences: Trip duration, style, and interest selections
  • Legal acceptance: A flag indicating you agreed to the disclaimer
  • Walkthrough dismiss: A flag indicating you've seen the onboarding
  • Install dismissal: A flag indicating you dismissed the install banner

Data We Do NOT Collect

  • ❌ No accounts or sign-ups
  • ❌ No analytics or tracking
  • ❌ No cookies
  • ❌ No server-side data storage
  • ❌ No third-party data sharing
  • ❌ No advertising SDKs

Network Requests

The app makes network requests only for:

  • City data files (JSON) — fetched from the same GitHub Pages origin
  • Map tiles — OpenStreetMap tiles (only when you activate Map View)
  • Google Maps links — only when you tap "Directions" (opens in a new tab)

Offline Operation

Once a city guide is loaded, it is cached by the service worker and works fully offline. Your favorites and preferences persist across sessions on the same device and browser.

Clearing Your Data

To clear all stored data: open the Help menu in the app and tap "Reset Local Settings & Restart Flow." You can also clear site data in your browser settings.

Children's Privacy

This app does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children under 13. No data is collected regardless of age.

Contact
