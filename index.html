Anatomy of a Local Venue Card
🗺️ Interactive Map Views & Route Maps

Tap "🗺️ Map View" in Browse to view all mapped venues on an interactive map. Inside your daily Itinerary, tap "📍 Map Stop" or "🗺️ Map Day Route" to view turn-by-turn walking/driving route lines with distance estimates.

🏘️ District & Neighborhood Filters

Select a city, then use the "District" dropdown in Browse to filter places specifically by that city's historic quarters and neighborhoods.

📤 Export & Sync Notes Between Devices

In Favorites or Help, tap "📤 Export Notes" to save a backup file. Import it on your computer or iPad to keep your trip notes and saved places in sync without requiring a cloud account!
